Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snootlog (“Snootlog,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Snootlog mobile application and related services (the “Service”).

Important: Snootlog helps you organize pet-care information. It is not a veterinary service and does not replace advice, diagnosis, or treatment from a qualified veterinarian.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, and account credentials or authentication information used to create and access your account.
  • Pet information: pet names, species, breed, age or date of birth, photos, and other details you choose to add.
  • Care records: medication names, dosage details, schedules, reminders, vaccination records, and related notes you enter.
  • Location information: approximate or precise device location, if you grant permission, to help show nearby veterinary clinics. You can control location permission through your device settings.
  • Device and app information: device type, operating system, app version, language, and diagnostic or usage information that may be provided by the device or service providers.
  • Communications: information you provide when contacting us for support or feedback.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information that is not needed for the pet-care features.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Save and display pet profiles, medication schedules, vaccination records, and care history.
  • Schedule and deliver medication or care reminders, where enabled.
  • Find and display nearby veterinary clinics when you choose to use location-based features.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the Service.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important service updates.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Location Permission

If you allow location access, Snootlog may use your device location to identify veterinary clinics near you. Location access is optional. You can deny or withdraw permission at any time in your device settings; location-based results may then be unavailable or less accurate.

4. Notifications and Reminders

Snootlog may request permission to send notifications for medication schedules and other reminders you configure. You can turn notifications off in your device settings. If you disable them, you may not receive scheduled reminders.

5. Photos and Camera

If you choose to add a pet photo, the app may request access to your photo library or camera. These permissions are used to let you select or take images for your pet profile. You can manage these permissions through your device settings.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: with vendors that help us operate the app, such as hosting, storage, analytics, crash reporting, or notification services. They may process information on our behalf under appropriate safeguards.
  • At your direction: when you choose to use a feature that sends information to another service.
  • Legal and safety reasons: where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, or our rights and the security of the Service.
  • Business changes: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to applicable law.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain your account and records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and how the Service is used.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may be able to access, correct, or delete information through the app. You can also manage location, camera, photo library, and notification permissions in your device settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights, such as requesting access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or objecting to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Children's Privacy

Snootlog is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age required by applicable law without appropriate consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we take steps intended to ensure that such transfers are subject to appropriate data-protection safeguards.

11. Third-Party Services and Links

The Service may use or link to third-party services, including map or veterinary-clinic information providers. Those services have their own privacy policies and practices. We encourage you to review them before providing information directly to those services.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and provide additional notice where required by law. Your continued use of the Service after an updated policy becomes effective means you acknowledge the revised policy.
